Scout Life Balance v2 is a game and training tool designed for scout leadership courses for future leaders and instructors. Participants make decisions through swipe-based dilemmas, see how their choices affect family, scouting, school, and energy, and use the game as a starting point for discussing how to protect life balance while serving and leading others.
